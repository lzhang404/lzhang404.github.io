Tree of Choice — Brute Force vs Backtracking

Make → Explore → Undo

Both brute force search and backtracking walk the same tree of choices. Brute force blindly explores every branch, while backtracking uses the recursive cycle of make a choice, explore, and undo to move between branches without rebuilding state.

Pick an example to see how the recursion tree captures the work: permutations for a shopping bag and ordered travel paths between cities. The stepper highlights the exact nodes that participate in the cycle.
